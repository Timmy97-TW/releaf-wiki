Dr. Pak K. Yuet, on adapting rather than inventing

18 April 2026. Adapt existing reactor designs rather than chase novelty.

Dr. Pak K. Yuet, on how to scale

16 July 2026. Scale on wall shear and flux below critical, not geometric similarity, and declare which similarity group is being sacrificed. We owe him a written justification for hollow-fibre over the packed-bed he recommended; that document belongs here.
