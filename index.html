01The threat

The weather swings hardest where farms are smallest.

Taiwan has warmed 0.29 °C per decade since 1991. We scored each county for how far its temperature and rainfall swing, then overlaid every farm parcel on the main island.1

But climate stress is not distributed evenly. To understand where farmers may be most exposed, we mapped climate variability alongside agricultural land.

The question was no longer just whether plants face stress, but whether protection can reach them at the right time.

02The gap

Protection exists. Reaching farmers in time is the challenge.

A commercial biological can take a decade to reach a farmer. Pivot Bio’s nitrogen-fixing microbes grew from university research begun around 2008, the company was founded in 2011, and its first product reached farms in 2019.3 In the EU, registration alone can take 7 to 9 years,2 and only four groups of organisms are admitted as microbial biostimulants.4

Product highlights

What a farm would actually receive

The reactor a farm would run, drawn from the CAD. Five properties decide its shape, and each one has a page behind it that says what is measured and what is still design intent.

  1. On-site

    Made beside the crop, so nothing is shipped, stored or held as inventory between the fermenter and the field.

  2. On-demand

    A run starts when the stress forecast calls for one, so quiet weeks cost nothing and nothing is made ahead to be thrown away.

  3. Precision agriculture

    Brightness sets the expression rate, so the dose follows the week the crop is having. Design intent: no induction curve has been measured yet.

  4. Biosecurity

    Engineered B. subtilis stays on its side of a 0.2 µm membrane. Shell-side medium plated on LB agar grew no colonies, in a single plating.

  5. Open-source

    Every drawing, model and part list is published under CC BY 4.0, so a farm, a school or another team can build one.

The synbio

A cell that responds to a color and releases the protectant.

ReLEAF uses the green-light-responsive CcaS/CcaR system to turn an external light signal into protectant production. Four modules carry that signal from light sensing to secretion.

Module 1PCB synthesis

ho1 and pcyA produce the pigment required for light sensing.

Module 2Light sensing

CcaS detects green or red light and changes its signaling state.

Module 3Transcription

Activated CcaS transfers the signal to CcaR, which turns on the output promoter.

Module 4Protectant output

The protectant is expressed and secreted into the medium.

Green light

Production ON

Red light

Production OFF

Cloning is modularised, so any gene B. subtilis can express drops into the output position without redesigning the circuit around it, including protectants we have not designed yet.

Our vision

We envision a future where every farmer has their own local biomanufacturing system.

We see ReLeaf as more than a bioreactor. The goal is to give farmers more control over how and when they protect their crops, by moving biological production closer to them instead of asking them to wait for it to arrive and worry about storage. Every farm already runs water to its plants, so the reactor is drawn to join the line that is there.

Each farm would operate a contained ReLEAF bioreactor that grows engineered B. subtilis and produces plant protectants on-site, so farmers can make biological protection where it is needed instead of waiting for it to move through a centralized supply chain.

The big picture

Fifteen components, five joints that have to close, and the people who moved them.

Read down. Five parts carry one dose from the weather to the crop: the stress the crop is under, the protein that answers it, the cells that make the protein, the bioreactor that holds the cells, and the field the dose has to reach. Three components sit inside each part, and under each part is the person whose advice changed it. Between the parts are the joints, and the joints are where this project is still open. Three of those four hand-overs have happened at least once. The fourth has not: nothing the reactor made has reached a plant. Nor has the loop back to the start, because no forecast has driven a light.

  1. 01

    The stress

    Heat and salt, on farms of about a hectare.

    • Farmland and climate map

      Almost every farm in Taiwan is under two hectares, the average farmer is 63.5 years old, and the weather swings hardest over exactly that ground.

      Mapped from open records

    • Stress index

      Temperature, salinity and humidity collapse into one number between 0 and 1, and that number sets how bright the green light should be.

      Computed, never yet driving a lamp

    • Plant stress assay

      Our own salt ladder. Mean root length at day 6 falls from 74.95 mm to 1.36 mm between no salt and 150 mM, nine seedlings a group.

      Measured, eight sets

    Prof. Cheng Mei-Chun gave us the seeds and the plant model, moved us off hydroponics as the main system, and capped the salt ladder at 200 mM for seedlings this young. What each conversation changed

  2. What passes: which protectant the crop needs. Heat and salinity were chosen with our advisers, and each protectant answers one of them. No protectant of ours has rescued a plant yet.

    Argued, not tested
  3. 02

    The protein

    Three protectants: ACC deaminase, BoPep4 and LEA14.

    • ACC deaminase

      The enzyme that lowers the ethylene a stressed plant makes. It is the protectant behind every bench result on this wiki. One fractionation blot puts it in the supernatant and in neither pellet.

      Made; no activity measured

    • BoPep4

      A twenty three residue plant elicitor peptide, docked into the PEPR1 groove and run for 270 ns of molecular dynamics. Six cassettes were ordered on 20 August 2026 and none has been expressed.

      Designed, not yet made

    • The design pipeline

      Alignment, docking, dynamics, then codon and signal peptide choice. An audit of our own method withdrew five claims in writing, one of them after it had already gone into a DNA order.

      Published with its retractions

    Dr. Elizabeth Sattely told us to test the purified protectant on plants before integrating it, and to focus on one stress. Dr. Jennifer Brophy suggested ACC deaminase in the first place. What each conversation changed

  4. What passes: a sequence the cell can express. Cargoes were ordered, cloned and sequence confirmed one unit at a time. The joined device is checked at its three junctions and has never been read end to end.

    Built, one check short
  5. 03

    The cells

    Bacillus subtilis, switched on by green light.

    • Bacillus subtilis 168

      Not a pathogen, and it secretes on its own, so nothing has to be broken open to get the product out. Every number on this wiki is strain 168.

      In use all year

    • The green light switch

      Four modules, pigment through to output: CcaS reads green light and CcaR turns the promoter on. Nine of thirteen units are sequence confirmed and joined into one plasmid. Nothing shows its output changing with light.

      Built, never induced

    • The control build

      Every light gated cargo was also built without the switch, so a protectant that fails to appear has one possible cause instead of two.

      Built alongside

    Prof. Huang Chieh-Chen told us that quantity, identity, solubility and function have to be evaluated together, and sent us home with the WB800 strain. What each conversation changed

  6. What passes: a culture the vessel has to keep alive. The strain carrying ACC deaminase without the switch grew in the loop for 53 hours. The light gated strain has never been in it.

    Run once, with the control strain
  7. 04

    The bioreactor

    The vessel that keeps the cells and lets the protein out.

    • Hollow fibre loop

      A perfusion loop around a 0.2 µm membrane. The cells live in the lumen and the protectant crosses into the harvest, so retention and product recovery are the same piece of hardware. Shell side medium plated clean, once.

      Built; held a culture 53 hours

    • Sensor suite

      One in line photometer, three pressure transducers and a flow meter log the run continuously. The pH and dissolved oxygen channels are not part of the batch record yet.

      Logging, in part

    • Digital twin

      A model of the unit that keeps the batch record, scores how far its own readings can be trusted, and decides when to induce. Anything it cannot undo waits for a person. Today it watches the rig and does not command it.

      Monitoring; the loop is not closed

    Dr. Jennifer Brophy moved us off a kill switch: containment should be physical, a membrane that keeps the bacteria and passes the protectant. Prof. Chang Jo-Shu added the foam breaker, the pressure watch and the replaceable capsule. What each conversation changed

  8. What would pass: a cell free permeate. Nothing the reactor has made has reached a plant. The reactor and the plants have run in the same incubator and have never been connected.

    Not made yet
  9. 05

    The field

    The farm the dose has to reach, and the rules it has to fit.

    • Delivery into water the farm already runs

      A bottled spray, or the irrigation and hydroponic lines a farm already has. The dose arithmetic is modelled end to end. No ReLeaf unit has fed either system.

      Modelled, not connected

    • The unit a farm would buy

      A reactor, a replaceable bacterial cartridge and a monitoring app. Large buyers and public funding carry the price so a smallholder or a cooperative can share one.

      Planned, not costed on a build

    • The rules it has to fit

      Taiwan first, then the European Union and Southeast Asia, traced part by part. Whether a ReLeaf protein counts as a plant growth auxiliary material has not been confirmed by the ministry.

      Asked, unanswered

    Ms. Chen Hui-Wen, who farms in Tamsui, set the ceiling on the dose: Sometimes, giving too much is not necessarily beneficial for a living organism; instead, it can take away its ability to survive and thrive on its own. What each conversation changed

  10. And back to the start: the next run. The forecast that reads the coming week is supposed to start the next batch, which is what makes this a loop rather than a line. No forecast has driven a light.

    Drawn, not wired

Two of these five joints have never been made: nothing the reactor produced has reached a plant, and no forecast has driven a light. That is the shape of the work left, and it is why this figure marks its joints instead of hiding them. Every component above links to the page that carries its evidence, and every one of those pages says where that evidence stops.

Not one of these fifteen components is the way we first drew it. A farmer, a plant scientist, a bioreactor engineer and a protectant manufacturer each moved one, and the membrane is here at all because an adviser argued us out of a kill switch. That is why the people are inside this figure rather than beside it.
