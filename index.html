01The threat

The weather swings hardest where Taiwan farms most.

Since 1991 the island has warmed 0.29 °C per decade, nearly triple its earlier rate. Our climate volatility index measures how far a place swings within a year: day against night, summer against winter, and how unevenly its rain falls.

Where the weather swings hardest

  • Yunlin to Tainan: the most volatile band
  • Taipei: 63 days above 35 °C in 2024
  • Kaohsiung: 19 °C from here to Jade Mountain

Where Taiwan farms

  • Western plain: most of the island’s farmland
  • Yilan: rice yield down to a third

Where the nights stay warm

  • Pingtung: evenings up to 27.26 °C
  • Hsinchu: the warmest band
  • Keelung: the warmest band

Most of the farmers we met had lost crops to environmental stress.

The damage starts before anyone can see it.

A forecast gives days of warning, but protection still has to be bought, mixed and sprayed in time. Protectants are made far from the farm and stored, and the right day to apply them is hard to judge before the crop shows anything.

Engineered cells have to stay out of the field.

Plant protectants are proteins and peptides that help a crop withstand heat, drought and salt, and bacteria can be engineered to make them.

The bacteria themselves must not reach the crop. Taiwan’s organic standards prohibit genetically modified preparations, and farmers at our forum asked whether a treated crop could still be certified organic. What a culture is making cannot be seen from outside either, so it has to be checked as it grows.

Current tools cover part of what the problem needs.

  • Ready-made protectants

    Chemical and biological products bought at a shop, some of them proteins made in bacteria that are killed before sale. They protect once someone has seen the risk, bought the product, mixed it and sprayed it.

    Covers Protective, Biosafe and Monitored.

  • Live microbial inoculants

    Living bacteria or fungi put on the seed or into the soil at planting, working from then on. Taiwan’s microbial fertilisers must be free of genetically modified microbes, which closes this route to an engineered strain.

    Covers Protective and Monitored.

  • On-farm biofactories

    Farms grow microbes in their own tanks and spray the broth when they need it. The tank is tended by hand, and the broth is a live culture whose quality rules are still being written.

    Covers Protective and On demand.

  • Alert and dosing platforms

    Soil sensors and forecasts that open a valve at the right hour, with nobody there. What the valve lets out is still the product someone bought and loaded.

    Covers On demand, Automatic, Biosafe and Monitored.

How can a farm protect its crops before the damage starts?

An answer has to meet five demands.

  • ProtectiveShields the crop from heat, drought and salt.
  • On demandMade when the forecast turns, before day 0.
  • AutomaticStarts without anyone mixing or spraying.
  • BiosafeNo engineered cell reaches the field.
  • MonitoredChecked while it is made, on the farm.
