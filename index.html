Every farmer a biomanufacturer.

This is about permission, not about owning a machine. The economic unit is the irrigation line and the owner is the cooperative. A farmer in a 產銷班 that owns a reactor is a biomanufacturer in the only sense that matters, which is that no outside licence-holder decides what gets made.

The claim

Three things this project is not

Agricultural biotechnology is usually sold on one of three stories. None of them is ours, and saying so early is the only way the rest of this wiki reads correctly.

Climate change

Climate is the weather the problem happens in. It is not the problem.

Farmer margins

A few percent on a harvest makes the project small, and it is not what is at stake.

Hunger

Hunger is mostly distribution and politics. It is not a yield problem waiting for a protein.

What is at stake is who is permitted to manufacture.

The build

A bioreactor that sits where the crop is

To get a useful protein today you have to buy it from somebody who owns a fermenter, a cold chain and a registration dossier. None of those three is a scientific obstacle, and none of them can be paid for across half a hectare. Smallholders are not served badly. They are structurally not served at all, by arithmetic rather than by anyone’s malice.

ReLeaf is a perfusion hollow-fibre membrane bioreactor small enough to stand next to the plants it serves, with an expression cassette that is a slot rather than a rebuild.

  1. The chassis Engineered B. subtilis 168, growing continuously in the extracapillary chamber, outside the fibres.
  2. The light switch 520 nm drives an optogenetic switch, so brightness is intended to set transcription rate. Design intent; no induction curve exists in our hands.
  3. The membrane Intended to hold every cell back and let protein through. The cutoff has not been chosen yet, and the two candidates are two different devices.
  4. The controller A local soil sensor and a weather forecast, meant to call heat, drought and pathogen conditions before the plant shows them.

Where the project actually stands

What is true, and what is not

Measured, in our hands

  • 3 wk of unbroken OD600 logging in the chamber, by an instrument we built. Unbroken describes the log, not the culture.
  • 15 design, build, test and learn cycles across the reactor, the photometer and the LED array.
  • 3 instruments built from nothing: the perfusion reactor, an in-line photometer, a dual-wavelength LED array.
  • 1 failure caught. Our photometer spotted the cross-flow pump dying before we did.

Not yet true

  • No biological output has been measured. No induction curve, no titre, no activity assay, and no plant has received anything.
  • We have not measured our own transfer function, so “brightness is dose” is design intent.
  • Cells do not grow in the fibre lumen, and we do not have a root cause.
  • Delivery stops at a reservoir. The second containment layer does not exist.
  • No bill-of-materials total, so every affordability claim would be a hypothesis. We do not make one.
  • Membrane cutoff is unchosen, and contained-use classification is unconfirmed by any Taiwan regulator.

This list is not exhaustive, and absence from it proves nothing. Each item is carried, with its evidence or its gap, on the page it belongs to: Results, Measurement, Bioreactor Calculations and Safety and Security.
