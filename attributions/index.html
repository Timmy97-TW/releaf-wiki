What iGEM asks for →

Three things go on this page and nothing else. The navigation menu, the footer, and the embedded attribution form. Do not add prose, photographs or a thank-you section here; iGEM is explicit that this page carries the standard form and nothing else. Fill the form in on teams.igem.org, start it early, and replace 0000 in the iframe src with the team number.
