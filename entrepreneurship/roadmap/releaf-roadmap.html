Execution principles

Evidence before deployment

Lab characterization, plant response and final-output safety data must connect before anything moves to a field.

Regulatory gates stay conditional

Product classification, contained-use obligations and field permissions are confirmed per jurisdiction and per intended use, never assumed from a neighbouring market.

Scale only after process validation

Controlled process engineering, equipment safety review and documented quality controls precede any manufacturing commitment.

Two schedules exist in the source

The narrative mentions Philippines entry in H2 of Year 2 and Thailand by Year 4, earlier than Phases 2 and 3. Both are carried as explicit reconciliation tasks rather than silently resolved.
