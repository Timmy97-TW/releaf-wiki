Review · Members, the page as a whole

Works. The bios sound like the people who wrote them, which is what a judge wants from this page. Chloe Wu’s line that iGEM is “building a foundation for how I think, collaborate with others” says what the year did for her in her own words, and Abigail Lin’s mystery-novel opening is a memorable way in. Listing each person’s tasks on the card gives the Attribution form something to agree with.

Fix. The page shows 46 people, but Milestone (“The forty-seven people are named on Team”) and the Team menu blurb (“The forty-seven of us”) say 47; Elizabeth Wong’s card was removed on purpose in commit 799ea92, so either change both to 46 or bring her card back. School names are only abbreviations (KCIS, KCISLK, TAS, IBSH, AAIA, WEGO, FPS, FHJH, TES); add one line spelling each out, since judges outside Taiwan will not know them. Dr. Pak appears here as “Dr. Pak” and in the Dry Lab Notebook as “Dr. Pak K. Yuet”; use one form, and give Timmy a surname if the other instructors have one. The Dry Lab Notebook spells Renee Kuo as “Renée Kuo”; ask Renee which is right. Nine student advisor cards have no bio, and Bruce Tsai and Dr. Pak have no portrait yet. The Student advisors note is built on “not just here to help out” and a five-item list; one plain sentence per advisor naming what they built would be stronger. The Support team section is empty and says so; remove it before the freeze unless someone is going to be listed. The key says “What a major means” while each card names a subteam; say whether the subteam on a card is that person’s major.
