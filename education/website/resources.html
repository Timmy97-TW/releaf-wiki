    Explores the biology of the Aedes aegypti mosquito, dengue virus replication, and how synthetic biology tools — genetic circuits, engineered bacteria, and ovitrap design — can support disease surveillance and prevention.

    Plant Stress

    Explores how climate change and environmental conditions can place plants under stress, from extreme heat and drought to diseases and other biological threats. Through hands-on activities, experiments, and interactive games, students learn how plants respond to stress and how synthetic biology can be used to understand and develop new approaches for protecting crops.
