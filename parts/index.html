Parts

Abstract

Parts is our collection of thirty-two entries in five modules: a green-light switch in Bacillus subtilis with a secreted plant protectant behind it.

Every evidence word in the table comes from the build record, a named colony and a date for sequence-confirmed; where gel and spreadsheet disagree the gel wins, and the three contested assemblies are named. Eighteen of the thirty-two entries are new this year, eight come from the SubtiToolKit and six reuse one of five distinct Registry accessions. Six entries are designs with no bench step recorded, two of them codon-optimised peptides confirmed at Level 0. The Level 2 join gave three clones with bands at all three junctions on 19 September, junction-checked and not sequenced. No part is characterised for light response: no induction curve, no dose response, no dark control.

The collection

The ReLEAF part collection: 32 parts in five modules, each with its iGEM Registry accession or its origin, its category, its type and the evidence behind it.
Function
Module 01 PCB production 3 parts The PCB Production Module supplies the chromophore required for light sensing. It consists of the ho1-pcyA operon, which converts endogenous heme into phycocyanobilin (PCB), the chromophore necessary for CcaS photoreceptor activity. PCB enables the light-sensing system to detect and respond to green light.
PtagC+RBSPCB production Kit Promoter + RBS Composite Sequence-confirmed L1

A standardized genetic part combining a bacterial promoter (PtagC) and a Ribosome Binding Site (RBS). This composite part provides control of both transcription and translation for a downstream gene of interest.
ho1-pcyAPCB production BBa_K1232004 ↗ Chromophore biosynthesis operon Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

The fusion protein of two enzymes, heme oxygenase-1 (Ho1) and phycocyanobilin:ferredoxin oxidoreductase (pcyA), is used for synthesizing phycocyanobilin (PCB).
L3S2P21PCB production Kit Terminator Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A strong, synthetic intrinsic (Rho-independent) transcription terminator derived from the synthetic terminator library of Chen et al. (2013).
Module 02 Light sensing 5 parts This module consists of the engineered sensor kinase CcaS-m3 driven by either the constitutive promoter PlepA or PliaG. When supplied with the chromophore phycocyanobilin (PCB) from the PCB Production Module, CcaS-m3 can detect green light and initiate signal transduction. Altogether, these components enable the conversion of external light stimuli into a regulatory signal for downstream gene expression.
PlepALight sensing BBa_K823002 ↗ Constitutive promoter Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A constitutive Bacillus subtilis promoter, derived from the lepA promoter region, that drives continuous transcription of downstream genetic elements.
PliaGLight sensing BBa_K823000 ↗ Constitutive promoter Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A weak constitutive promoter from Bacillus subtilis that drives continuous, low-level baseline transcription of downstream genes.
RBS0Light sensing Kit RBS Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

RBS0 is a synthetic, high-strength ribosome binding site optimized for Bacillus subtilis. It promotes efficient translation initiation of the CcaS-m3 photoreceptor.
ccaS-m3Light sensing New Photoreceptor Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

The miniaturized optogenetic variant of the CcaS in the cyanobacterial CcaS/CcaR system. Under green light, it autophosphorylates and undergoes phosphotransfer to the response regulator CcaR.
B0015Light sensing Kit Terminator Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A high-efficiency double strong transcriptional terminator in E. coli and B. subtilis that stops RNA polymerase from continuing transcription past the ho1-pcyA operon, preventing transcriptional read-through into downstream modules.
Module 03 Transduction 4 parts This module consists of CcaR, the response regulator component of the cyanobacterial CcaS/CcaR two-component signaling system. Acting as the intermediary between light perception and gene expression, CcaR receives the phosphoryl group from the green light-activated CcaS and activates transcription from the PcpcG2 promoter. Together, it enables the conversion of environmental light cues into a transcriptional response.
PvegTransduction BBa_K143012 ↗ Constitutive promoter Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A constitutive bacterial promoter originally derived from Bacillus subtilis that drives continuous gene transcription.
RBS0Transduction Kit RBS Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

RBS0 is a synthetic, high-strength ribosome binding site optimized for Bacillus subtilis. It promotes efficient translation initiation of the CcaR response regulator.
CcaRTransduction BBa_K592002 ↗ Response regulator Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

This OmpR-class response regulator protein receives phosphate groups from activated CcaS and activates transcription from the light-responsive promoter PcpcG2.
B0015Transduction Kit Terminator Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A high-efficiency double transcriptional terminator that stops RNA polymerase from continuing transcription past the CcaR response regulator, preventing transcriptional read-through into downstream modules.
Module 04 Green-light responsive expression 6 parts This module includes the light-responsive promoter PcpcG2-veg and an EEBD, which together drive the expression of downstream genes in response to green-light signaling in Bacillus subtilis. Multiple CDS were evaluated, including different protectant candidates: AcdS, LEA and BoPEP4, each fused to either the Csn or SamyQ secretion signal and a His-tag. Together, these components convert light-induced regulatory signals into measurable visual outputs or stress-mitigation functions.
PcpcG2-vegGreen-light expression New Light-responsive promoter Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

The synthetic PcpcG2-veg hybrid promoter is structurally composed of an upstream PcpcG2 operator region fused to a downstream Pveg core motif. The upstream segment originates from the promoter of cpcG2 in Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803, which encodes a phycobilisome rod-core linker protein and contains the CcaR binding motif. The downstream segment is derived from a native, highly robust Bacillus subtilis promoter Pveg, contributing a high-affinity −10 hexamer and the sequence immediately downstream of it.
EEBD-RBS0Green-light expression New RBS Composite Sequence-confirmed L1

A composite ribosome binding site element containing a synthetic RBS sequence to improve translation initiation efficiency. EEBD is the Escherichia coli expression blocking device, consisting of a B0015 terminator between two recombination sites to prevent unwanted gene expression, including the production of toxic proteins, during cloning of DNA constructs in Escherichia coli.
Csn-AcdS-His6Green-light expression New CDS Composite Sequence-confirmed L1

Encodes ACC deaminase (AcdS), an enzyme that degrades 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate (ACC), the precursor of ethylene, thereby promoting plant growth under stress conditions. It is fused to Csn, an N-terminal signal peptide from Bacillus subtilis used to direct secretory expression.
SamyQ-AcdSGreen-light expression New Functional gene cassette Composite Designed

A gene construct encoding ACC deaminase (AcdS), an enzyme involved in reducing ethylene levels by degrading 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate (ACC), fused to the SamyQ signal peptide from Bacillus subtilis, forming a secreted enzyme fusion protein.
SamyQ-AcdS v2t1Green-light expression New Functional gene cassette Composite Sequence-confirmed L1

A gene construct encoding ACC deaminase, an enzyme involved in reducing ethylene levels by degrading 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate (ACC), fused to the SamyQ signal peptide from Bacillus subtilis, forming a secreted enzyme fusion protein. Codon optimization was carried out on the original wild type to stabilize molecule structure during field application to improve and maximize protein efficiency.
B0015Green-light expression Kit Terminator Basic Sequence-confirmed L1

A high-efficiency double transcriptional terminator that stops RNA polymerase from continuing transcription past the CDS protectant, preventing transcriptional read-through into downstream modules.
Module 05 Expression 14 parts This system includes the protectant CDS fused to secretion and detection tags. Designed as a reporter construct, it enables visualization of gene expression while supporting the evaluation of protein secretion and localization. These components provide a convenient tool for characterizing protein export within the system.

Key

BBa_… ↗
A green accession is a link: it opens that part's entry on the iGEM Registry in a new tab. Six rows carry one, holding five distinct accessions, all of them from earlier teams.
Kit
Taken from the SubtiToolKit [5], the Golden Gate kit this collection is built in, which has no Registry accession of its own.
New
Built by ReLEAF this year and not yet carrying an accession. Eighteen of the thirty-two entries, and what we would submit.
Composite · Basic
Composite parts are assembled from parts that stand on their own; basic parts are not divisible into smaller functional parts.
Sequence-confirmed L1
A named colony and a sequencing date: somebody read the DNA back. L1 is a whole transcription unit, L0 an entry part.
Designed
A sequence exists, in most cases a synthesis plasmid with it, and no bench step after that is recorded.
