Parts

Abstract

Thirty-five parts registered on the iGEM Registry this year: a green-light switch in Bacillus subtilis with a secreted plant protectant behind it.

The collection is built in three levels. Level 0 holds the three protectant proteins with their recoded variants, the eight fusions that add a secretion signal and a His tag, and four regulatory elements. Level 1 puts a fusion behind a promoter: eight constitutive protectant cassettes and three on the green-light promoter, beside the three units that make the switch. Level 2 joins all four modules in one shuttle vector. Three entries carry characterisation, Western blots of His-tagged ACC deaminase, and eighteen carry the build and how it was verified.
