Description

Biomanufacturing is gated, and the gate is not made of science. ReLeaf is an attempt to hand the means of production to the person standing in the field.

On this page

The problem we picked

Open on who is structurally not served and why the arithmetic, not anyone's malice, puts them there. Use the sourced context figures from the project brief. Do not let this drift into climate, margins or hunger.

Why we did not pick the obvious problem

State plainly what ReLeaf is not about: not climate change, not farmer margins, not hunger. Name what is at stake instead, which is who is permitted to manufacture.

What ReLeaf is

One paragraph a non-specialist can repeat back. Then the system in four moving parts.

The chassis

Engineered B. subtilis 168, growing continuously in the extracapillary chamber. Use the compartment vocabulary exactly: chamber outside the fibres, lumen is the bore.

The light switch

520 nm, CcaS/CcaR driving P_cpcG2. Brightness is intended to set transcription rate. Mark it as design intent, in the same sentence, at the same size.

The membrane

What it is intended to retain and what it is intended to pass. Cutoff is unchosen; say so here rather than leaving it to the calculations page.

The controller

Local sensor plus forecast, and what it is meant to call before the plant shows it.

Why it had to be alive

Answer the sachet. A dried spore sachet is ambient-stable, cheap and real, and shelf life alone argues for it. The refusal is dose and retasking, not shelf life.

What is true today

Only the measured facts, in the team's own hands. Three weeks of unbroken OD600 logging, fifteen design cycles, three instruments built, one failure caught by our own photometer.

What is not true yet

Put the limits on the page before a judge finds them. This list is not exhaustive and absence from it proves nothing.

Not yet demonstrated

No biological output has been measured: no induction curve, no titre, no activity assay, and no plant has received anything. Every statement about dose on this page is design intent.

Where this goes next

The next design decision, not a wish list. The membrane cutoff is the central unmade decision; say what would settle it.
