01December 2025 to April 2026

Firsts

At the first meeting there was no project yet, and one member had already brought a microcontroller. Over the next four months each subteam took its own first step.

On the wiki: Milestone / Human Practices / Development

0216 May 2026

One Saturday

On 16 May the team split up. At the farmers’ market, growers told us their problems were weather, then water, then price. Back in the team room the advisors sat down with the dry lab, and in the lab the Arabidopsis seedlings were up on agar.

On the wiki: Human Practices / Plants / Dry Lab Notebook

0327 May to 23 August 2026

First light

The dry lab built an in-line photometer to read the density of the bacteria without opening the loop. Wet lab students switched it on the first time, and the wet lab’s BioDrop became the instrument it was checked against.

On the wiki: In-Line Photometer / Perfusion Bioreactor / Dry Lab Notebook

0427 May to 19 September 2026

Green on, red off

The wet lab’s light switch turns protectant production on under green light and off under red. To test it, the dry lab built DiOPAL: 24 LEDs, six light conditions in one run. In September the wet lab ran its first assay on it.

On the wiki: DiOPAL / Measurement / Milestone

0525 April to 12 September 2026

The cartridge

A hollow-fibre membrane cartridge sits at the centre of the bioreactor. The bacteria stay on one side and the protectant they make crosses to the other. Much of what was built around it started as advice from outside the team.

On the wiki: Perfusion Bioreactor / Human Practices / Hardware Notebook

0627 March to 28 August 2026

Roots in water

Prof. Cheng showed us seedlings growing in plain plastic boxes. The wet lab spent the summer learning what kills them. After a hydroponic farm visit in August, the dry lab went straight back to its drawings of the floating plate.

On the wiki: Plants / Hydroponics Floating Plate / Human Practices

0725 June to 22 August 2026

The same green

Grind a leaf, add acetone, and the green comes out into the tube. The wet lab uses that extraction to read plant stress; human practices carried the same extraction into primary school classrooms.

On the wiki: Plants / Education / Wet Lab Notebook

089 July to 15 August 2026

How much to give

On her farm Ms. Chen told us that giving a plant too much can take away its ability to thrive on its own. How much protectant, and delivered how, then came up at CH Biotech, in the dry lab’s notes with Dr. Pak, and on the wet lab’s plates.

On the wiki: Human Practices / Plants / Dry Lab Notebook

097 August to 5 September 2026

Everything in one room

At the Field Dialogue forum on 5 September the reactor, the stress map and our talks stood side by side for the first time, in front of farmers, experts and more than fifty visitors. Each piece was finished in the weeks before by a different part of the team.

On the wiki: Human Practices / Data Physicalization / Milestone
