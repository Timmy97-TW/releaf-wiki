Experiments

Abstract

Experiments collects the 25 protocol sheets behind ReLEAF’s wet-lab work, organized across four experimental stages and paired with downloadable procedures and diagrams.

The sheets follow four stages: ten for cloning, eight for protein production, five plant assays and two for the bioreactor. All but one carry the full procedure as a downloadable PDF, down to the reagent volumes and the arm the readout is scored against. The newest is the His-tag detection ELISA, which reads His-tagged protein against a six-point standard curve. Activity is measured instead by the DNPH assay, scaled down twenty-fold onto a 96-well plate. Three sheets produced no result on this wiki: the Bradford assay, large scale extraction, and purification and dialysis.

Stages

  • CloningTen sheets: PCR, Golden Gate, transformation, screening
  • Protein productionEight sheets: extraction, SDS-PAGE, blot, ELISA, DNPH
  • Plant assaysFive sheets: agar salt, heat, soil, hydroponics, chlorophyll
  • BioreactorTwo sheets: a perfusion run and the silica OD600 calibration

On this page

Overview

ReLEAF makes plant protectants in Bacillus subtilis and tests them on Arabidopsis under salt and heat stress. The wet lab work runs in four stages, and each hands its product to the next: cloning builds the constructs, protein production gets the protectant out of the culture and measures its activity, the plant assays test it, and the bioreactor sheets calibrate the instruments that watch the culture grow.
