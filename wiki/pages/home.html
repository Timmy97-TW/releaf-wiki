How ReLEAF works

  1. Predict local stress A leaf sensor monitors leaf temperature and wetness, which combines with the weather forecast. Our predictive AIoT (artificial intelligence of things) model turns the data into a stress index and predicts when stress will become too high for the crop, so the farmer sees it on the ReLEAF app and can prepare ahead.
  2. Green light activation When predicted stress approaches the threshold, the farmer approves the run on the app and the system activates 520 nm green light, switching on the CcaSR optogenetic circuit in engineered B. subtilis.
  3. Produce the protectant The engineered B. subtilis responds to the light signal by producing plant-protective proteins inside the bioreactor.
  4. Retain the bacteria, release the product The culture circulates through a hollow-fiber membrane that retains the engineered bacteria while allowing smaller soluble protectants to pass into the harvest bottle.
  5. Collect, dilute, spray The farmer collects the protectant from the harvest bottle, dilutes it with water by the factor the app states, and sprays it on the crop by hand, before the stress reaches the crop. Spraying by hand is how the system is used now; delivery through irrigation is a possibility for later. No laboratory skills are needed.
  6. Switch off production Once the predicted stress has passed, 660 nm red light switches the circuit off again, reducing unnecessary protein production and metabolic burden.

Green light switches on the CcaSR circuit in Bacillus subtilis, and the cells secrete the protectant
