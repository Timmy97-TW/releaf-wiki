# ReLEAF 中文術語表 · ReLEAF Chinese glossary

The wiki is written in English. The Chinese reading beside it is **zh-Hant,
Taiwan usage** — 枯草桿菌 not 枯草芽孢杆菌, 質體 not 质粒, 酵素 not 酶, 軟體 /
硬體 / 韌體 not 软件 / 硬件 / 固件. A page that drifts into mainland
terminology reads as a machine translation to the people this wiki is for.

Rules that hold on every page:

- **Names stay in Latin script.** ReLEAF, BoPep4, AtLEA14, ACCD, CcaSR, WB800,
  iGEM, GEMS Taiwan, part numbers, software names, people's names.
- **Numbers, units and chemical formulae stay as they are** — 24 h, 3.4 µg/mL,
  OD600, °C, SiO₂ — with a space between the number and a Latin unit, and no
  space before a Chinese character. 「12% 的綠光」, not 「12%的綠光」.
- **A term's first use on a page may carry the English in brackets**:
  數位孿生（digital twin）. Afterwards the Chinese alone.
- **Translate the claim, never soften or sharpen it.** A hedge in the English
  ("suggests", "not yet tested", "n = 1") is load-bearing evidence language and
  must survive in the Chinese. Do not add a conclusion the English does not make.
- **Keep the markup.** A translation carries the same `<b>`, `<a href>`,
  `<sup>` and `<abbr>` as the English it replaces.
