Source: the students' Drive export of 1 October 2026, unpacked to
`drafts/2026-10-01 student drafts/` with plain text in its `_text/` folder.

Two jobs, in this order: bring in the students' newest writing as their own black
prose, and cut the assistant's orange back to what the pages genuinely need. The
homepage and `team/` were out of scope and are untouched.

## Pages that stayed orange on purpose

Engineering (69.8%), Milestone (46.9%), Results (84.2%), Digital Twin (91.6%),
Protein Design sprints (86.5%) and molecular dynamics (76.1%), Gallery (45.3%).

In each case there is no student draft behind the page, or the draft is an
outline rather than prose. The orange on them is the only copy of a DBTL record,
a dated result, a figure caption or a photo story. Deleting it would delete the
content, not the drafting. Engineering's student file is one byte.

Inclusivity was not touched at all. It is still an unwritten scaffold with its six
scaffold notes intact, which is more honest than filling it.

## Decisions worth knowing about

**Measurement.** The DNPH activity assay block became the Berthelot assay, because
DNPH could not be bought and Berthelot is the assay the team actually ran. The
block moved from Design only to Partly run, and the run/design counts elsewhere on
the page moved with it: ten run, four design-only.

**Parts.** The students' Registry write-ups correct the page: the v2 series is a
synonymous codon recoding to open the mRNA start site, not a point mutation to
stabilise the fold. Six card descriptions were rewritten from their sentences.
The page stays an index, with the new text inside the existing folds.

**Plants.** "Vernalization" became "stratification" in three places, phase 3 is
ACCD, LEA and BoPep4 rather than ACCD alone, and germination is 7 days.

**DiOPAL.** The printed-parts table says Black PLA for `Tube_Holder`, not White.
A BOM a reproducer prints from should not contradict the students' newest writing,
which is that they reprinted it black at four wall loops after the white one
leaked light between holes.

**References.** Each page's own citations were appended after the existing ones on
Measurement, Parts and Plants. `assets/js/page.js` links `[n]` by position, so
appending is the only safe way to add them: the original 9, 5 and 10 entries are
preserved exactly as a prefix and nothing renumbered. `audit.py` still reports
`cite 0`.

## Open items this pass surfaced

- **Two numbers now disagree on their own pages.** On Integrated Human Practices
  the students' new forum text gives the general public 90 / 95 / 100 for
  knowledge, attitude and practice, where the chart above it shows 93 / 100 / 100.
  On Math Model the new draft says the model crosses five physically distinct
  domains, where the existing block says four and names four. Student words were
  taken as written in both cases and the figures left alone.
- **The bioreactor page gained five pending chips.** The students wrote their
  tubing runs as `A (_), B (_), C (_)`. Those blanks are now visible rather than
  absent. They need measuring before the freeze.
- **Student writing with no page to hold it.** The Description draft carries a
  bibliometric trend paragraph, a *B. thuringiensis* techno-economic analysis and
  a cost and vaccine-transport comparison. The Contribution draft carries
  write-ups for the continuous-flow bioreactor and DiOPAL. The Biomanufacturing
  draft carries two section outlines implying a much larger page. None of it was
  invented into new sections, because that is restructuring rather than writing.
- **The Digital Twin draft is last round's Software Content doc renamed**, and it
  is an outline with notes to the student rather than prose. Its section 3, the
  decision ladder with the Tier 0 to 3 autonomy table, which the outline itself
  calls the novel section, is still unwritten.
- **Photometer** still has 36 pending chips and three unwritten sections
  (Measurement Principle, Path Length, Calibration Procedure). Nothing new arrived
  for them and no orange was written into them.

## Method

Seven agents, one per page group, each in its own git worktree with strict file
ownership, merged into `main` and pushed in seven increments. `build/audit.py`
reported 0 for missing, anchor, cite, dupid, alt and parse before the pass and
reports the same after.
