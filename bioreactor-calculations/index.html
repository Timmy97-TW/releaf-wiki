Bioreactor Calculations

Sizing, flow and mass transfer for a hollow-fibre vessel small enough to sit where the crop is.

Contents

On this page

Design brief and constraints

Volume, footprint, power, and what is fixed versus what is chosen. Reactor dimensions are not on record; settle them first.

Geometry and volumes

Fibre count, length, inner diameter, packing fraction, extracapillary volume. Use the compartment vocabulary exactly.

Flow, shear and residence time

Cross-flow rate, wall shear at the fibre surface, and the residence time distribution.

Mass transfer and oxygen

Oxygen is usually what limits a dense culture. Show the calculation and the margin.

Membrane selection

0.2 micron and 50 kDa are not two candidate values, they are two different devices with opposite consequences. Work both and say which one makes 'passes only protein' true.

The unmade decision

Membrane cutoff is unchosen. This is the central open design decision in the project, not a discrepancy in the notes.

Scale-up rules

Scale on wall shear and flux below critical, not geometric similarity, and declare which similarity group is being sacrificed. Credit the advice on the Human Practices page.

Worked example

One vessel, all the way through, with units at every line.

Assumption register

Every number used above that is not measured.
