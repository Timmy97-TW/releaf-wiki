Dry Lab Notebook

Four pipelines, four frames. The OD600 photometer, BoPep4 held against the PEPR1 receptor, the light plate apparatus on the bench, and the bioreactor running in the incubator.

Our project followed an iterative Design-Build-Test-Learn (DBTL) cycle that guided the development of both computational and engineering solutions. We began by designing approaches through literature analysis and identifying key challenges within our system, which led to the creation of specialized pipelines and design strategies. We then built these workflows by integrating computational models, analytical methods, and supporting engineering components to address specific research questions. Through testing, we evaluated our predictions, measurements, and system performance, while learning from each result to refine our models, optimize our designs, and generate new directions. This continuous cycle allowed individual ideas to evolve into interconnected pipelines, from biological analysis and computational validation to the development of supporting hardware and automated systems.
