Experiments

Abstract

Experiments collects the protocol sheets behind the wet lab work, grouped into four stages, each sheet as a PDF beside its procedure diagram.

The sheets follow four stages in the order one hands its product to the next: ten for cloning, six for protein production, five plant assays and two for the bioreactor. All but one carry the full procedure as a downloadable PDF, down to the reagent volumes, the cycling conditions and the blank or untreated arm the readout is scored against. Three produced no result on this wiki: the Bradford assay, large scale extraction, and purification and dialysis. Electroporation ran on both strains in the sheet: B. subtilis WB800 gave countable colonies at 2.1 to 2.5 kV, strain 168 only a handful.

On this page

Overview

ReLEAF makes plant protectants in Bacillus subtilis and tests them on Arabidopsis under salt and heat stress. The wet lab work runs in four stages, and each hands its product to the next: cloning builds the constructs, protein production gets the protectant out of the culture, the plant assays test it, and the bioreactor sheet calibrates the instruments that watch the culture grow.
