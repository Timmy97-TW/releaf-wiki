Taiwan fertilizer supply routes: suppliers to farmland, 19 counties

2.79 million farmland parcels (743,529 ha) are aggregated into 346 township-level demand nodes. 616 registered fertilizer and agrochemical suppliers are mapped by address. Routes follow the real road network (OSRM) and stay within each supplier's own county.

Cost and emission parameters

Truck operating cost
$1.45 USD / km
Emission factor
0.276 kg CO₂e / t·km
  1. Cost: US$2.336 per mile (2025 average), converted to US$1.45/km. American Transportation Research Institute, An Analysis of the Operational Costs of Trucking: 2026 Update. Covers fuel, wages, maintenance, tolls, insurance and truck payments. It is a US benchmark because no per-km truck cost is published for Taiwan, so treat it as an upper estimate.
  2. Emissions: 7.5–16 t ambient-temperature truck freight service at an 80% load factor, including depot emissions. Ministry of Environment (Taiwan) carbon footprint emission factor database, coefficient declared by the MOTC Institute of Transportation (2017).
Cost is for one truck's round trip (2 × one-way distance). The emission factor is per tonne-kilometre of cargo, so emissions are shown per tonne of fertilizer delivered (one-way distance × factor). Reporting it this way avoids assuming a truck payload. The empty return leg is not counted.
