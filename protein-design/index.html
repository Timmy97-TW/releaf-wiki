Protein Design

Abstract

This section is the dry lab work that chose ReLeaf’s three protectants and decided what to change about BoPEP4 before any DNA was ordered.

Only BoPEP4 was simulated, the one protectant with a solved receptor complex to work from. Its alignment puts 10 of 23 residues identical to AtPEP1, all in the contact region; the opening GILIGS segment comes off with the docking score flat, which is where the truncation comes from. Six histidines after Asn23 passed the project’s own acceptance gate and then held the Arg487 salt bridge in no frame, because the gate was written before the construct existed. Every sequence was then written into DNA for Bacillus subtilis, where recoding the signal peptide’s first 31 codons cleared the mRNA structure over the ribosome binding site that recoding the passenger gene alone had left standing. Nothing here has met a plant, and the ranking score did not track structural accuracy in our own validation.
